Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, bank links, and refunds.

Is there a contract or commitment with Veloce?

No contract whatsoever. Veloce is a pay-as-you-go service with either monthly or annual billing frequencies. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any point directly from the application account settings screen.

How safe is the automated bank linking?

Unbelievably secure. We utilize Plaid, the industry gold standard for API connections, which handles bank verification for major applications globally. Your credentials never touch Veloce servers. Your data is read-only, meaning Veloce can never move money; it only analyzes spending habits.

What happens once my 14-day free trial concludes?

You will receive a brief reminder notification email 3 days before your trial period completes. If you wish to proceed with automated tracking, your payment method on file will be billed for the plan frequency selected. Otherwise, you can easily switch down to the free tier manually.
