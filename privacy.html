1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to Veloce AI (the "Application" or "Platform"), owned and operated by Veloce Technologies, Inc. ("Veloce", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy details our unwavering commitment to processing the financial details you entrust to our smart budgeting and auto-tracking services with absolute discretion, state-of-the-art security, and transparent methodologies.

Our core mission is to help you track spending, organize savings into logical buckets, and automatically keep track of your cash flows using state-of-the-art Artificial Intelligence tools. Because our application maps to your active banking credentials, card platforms, and dynamic transactional patterns, we employ security protocols engineered to prevent unauthorized access, ensuring you retain 100% data sovereignty.

Key Principle: Under no circumstances does Veloce AI maintain credentials to manipulate your bank deposits, transfer liquidity, or modify balances. All data streaming pipelines to our servers are governed exclusively as read-only.

3. Automated AI Tracking & AI Systems Protocols

Veloce AI is upgraded with an autonomous categorization engine. Here is specifically how our algorithmic layer handles your tracking:

  • Local Metadata Sifting: Transaction details derived from linked cards are fed through isolated preprocessing steps. The actual text values are analyzed solely to match purchase names (e.g., "Starbucks") with target bucket parameters (e.g., "Coffee").
  • No Public Model Leakage: We strictly refrain from feeding transaction logs, names, locations, or account numbers into public generative AI frameworks. All algorithmic mapping is performed inside isolated private virtual clouds.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Override: When you manually change a category bucket, our AI marks this edit locally. This reinforces your dedicated individual model and does not create public exposure of your transactions.

4. Bank & Plaid Integration Standards

To fetch transaction schedules smoothly without risk, Veloce AI utilizes Plaid, the global gold standard in secure banking infrastructure.

How your Bank credentials remain invisible to us:

When you input banking credentials in Veloce AI, you are communicating directly with your institution via Plaid's secure portal. Veloce AI never sees, tracks, saves, or stores your plain-text bank password or username.

Once authentication succeeds, Plaid feeds our system an encrypted, read-only token to retrieve transactions. You can revoke this credential flow anytime through the connection settings page inside the Veloce AI application.
